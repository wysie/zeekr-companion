## Zeekr Companion 2.11.55-alpha.1

Download the `.apk` below and sideload it through App Lab.

## What changed

- Add Companion settings export/import, including camera profiles, vehicle/GPU choices and 360° calibration/window settings, with restore on reopening and rollback protection.
- Add Lock current recording to floating-button tap, double tap and long press, protecting clips covering ten seconds before and after across all recording sources.
- Add System → GPU image processing with Off, All and Custom choices, preserving existing GPU selections and independent Straighten controls.
- Add shared per-pixel GPU rendering for blind-side popup/HUD, Super mirror and the blind-camera settings preview, with compatibility rendering retained.
- Use the same portrait half-screen expansion for the 360° window, with exact size and position restoration.
- Expand the blind-side camera into a portrait half-screen panel regardless of its saved aspect ratio, with exact window restoration.
- Preserve native camera detail in the Blind spot camera settings preview instead of shrinking each side image to about 510 pixels before correction.
- Put Selected recording sources first in the preview quick presets, followed by the existing three presets.
- Match the navigation drawer to the neutral Settings styling and add a live 360° window switch beneath Super mirror.

## Getting started

Plug in a USB drive.

Something wrong? Export a report from **Settings → System → Diagnostics** and attach it to an issue.

## Safety

Experimental, unofficial software. Not affiliated with, approved by, or endorsed by ZEEKR, and not certified for any vehicle safety function.

- Try anything new in a stationary vehicle first.
- It does not replace the factory dash cam, reversing camera or blind-spot monitor.
- Do not operate it while driving, and do not judge distances or obstacles from its picture.
- It competes with the head unit for resources and **may** affect factory features; stop using it and uninstall if anything behaves oddly.
- Recordings on the USB drive are not encrypted and can contain faces and plate numbers. Follow your local law.
